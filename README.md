# Overview

This lab analyzes the BRAND_A TV advertising campaign in a synthetic US panel during October 2024. It uses commercial logs, rate cards, viewing sessions, respondent attributes, and daily weights to measure ad load, campaign cost, impressions, GRPs, reach, and frequency across 10 networks. The analysis compares P2+ and A18-49 audiences, evaluates CPM and CPP, checks the campaign guarantee, and examines audience overlap and panel uncertainty.
